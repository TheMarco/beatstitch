BeatStitch — First mix demo

Three original, procedurally synthesized dance recordings made for this project.
No external recordings, samples or loops are used. You may use this audio in demo
mixes, exports and shared test projects. The generator is build_demo_assets.py.

Each song is 32 bars of 4/4: 8 bars of drums, 8 bars with bass/chords/lead,
4 bars of breakdown, 4 bars returning to the groove, and 8 bars of drums.
The musical layer releases before the drums-only ending. BPM, key and beat grids
come from the composition; this project does not measure analyzer accuracy.
The three-track mix plays at 126 BPM with eight-bar bass-handoff transitions.
Pitch and optional blend-level balancing start off. All choices remain editable.
