DJ Studio keeps only inference, uses an equivalent torch FFT frontend,
and aggregates AudioSet classes into descriptive passage observations.
It does not retrain the network or claim that its scores are calibrated
probabilities, that absent tags prove absent voices, or that drum-led audio
is free of pitched bass, melody, vocals or reverb tails.
